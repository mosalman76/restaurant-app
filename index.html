Our Story

Food Made With
Love Since 1987

Bella Cucina was born in a small kitchen in Rome, where Chef Marco Rossi learned the art of cooking from his grandmother. Every recipe carries decades of tradition, using only the freshest seasonal ingredients sourced from local farms.

Today our family restaurant serves hundreds of guests every week, but the soul remains the same — honest, generous Italian cooking that makes you feel at home.
